# Chapter 16 colour plan

Prepared 2026-10-05 with `config.md`. Colour reaches these pages in root
`RULES.md` item 7's four ways, fact, convention, referent and category, and
where two apply the earlier wins. This file records what is particular to the
chapter; it invents no hue.

## Categories

| Quantity | Type | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| S, ΔS, S°, ΔS°, ΔS_univ, ΔS_sys, ΔS_surr, S_f, S_i, S_solid, S_liquid, S_gas | `entropy` | The chapter's own hue: every entropy axis, bar and readout wears it; standard values are told by their ° mark, initial and final by weight |
| G, ΔG, ΔG°, ΔG_f°, ΔG₁°, ΔG₂°, ΔH, ΔH°, ΔH_f°, H, ΔU, q, q_rev, q_surr, q_sys, q_X, q_Y, w, w_max, and TΔS once multiplied by T | `energy` | Where ΔH, TΔS and ΔG are drawn on one ladder or one plot they differ by dashing and label, never by hue (book `COLOR.md`) |
| T, T_X, T_Y, T_sys, T_surr, a temperature axis | `temperature` | The slider of 16.13 and 16.11; never a warm-to-cold tint on a body |
| K, K_P, K_sp, Q, Q_P | `equilibrium-constant` | 16.4 only, where ΔG° meets K |
| P (16.1, the empty flask), partial pressures in 16.4 examples | `pressure` | Where a readout states one |
| ΔV | `volume` | 16.1's expansion |
| Time on the axes of Figures 16.2, 16.4 and 16.5, the time t on Figure 16.2's slider | `time` | — |
| 1.00 kJ/K, the heat capacity of Figure 16.5's objects | `heat-capacity` | In the caption and in Figure 16.5's readout |

These stay in ink: W, W_f, W_i (counts of microstates), k (the Boltzmann
constant), N and n (numbers of particles and boxes), ν (stoichiometric
coefficients), R, ln Q and ln K as terms, a percent remaining, a probability.

A word or phrase wears a category when it names the concept: "entropy",
"the entropy change of the universe", "standard entropy", "free energy
change", "the standard free energy of formation of HgO". A law, a rule or a
skill (the second law, predicting the sign of ΔS) stays ink, and so does
"spontaneous" and every word about spontaneity.

## Facts and conventions

`F.el` for every atom and molecule drawn: the argon of Figure 16.4 and the
water of Figure 16.10 + 16.11, whose kinetic energy axis is `energy`. The
four particles of Figure 16.8 are told apart by `F.cat`, since the text names
none of them; Example 16.2's image is the book's own. The energy units of Figure 16.9 are ink marks, the book's asterisks.

## Referents

Each section lists its own in `referents`. As built:

- **16.1.** `tc-99m` and `u-238`, one curve each on Figure 16.2; `object-x`
  and `object-y`, the two blocks of Figure 16.5 and their temperature curves,
  with T_X, T_Y, q_X and q_Y split so that each subscript wears its object's
  colour (`ref` on their variables rows). The flasks of Figure 16.4 are not
  referents: the count of atoms in each is one line on the graph beneath,
  the left flask's in `F.cat(2)` and the right's in `F.cat(1)`, named in the
  legend.
- **16.2.** `hot-object` and `cold-object`, the two objects of Figure 16.9,
  their particles' letters and every mention of either in the text. The four
  particles of Figure 16.8 are instances (`F.cat`), not referents.
- **16.3.** None. The Sim draws the system's, the surroundings' and the
  universe's entropy changes as three bars of one category, told apart by
  fill (hollow for the system and surroundings, solid for the universe) and by
  label, and draws neither the ice nor its surroundings as a body.
- **16.4.** None. Each figure draws one line or one curve; the four cases of
  Figure 16.12 sit in the table beside the graph, the current one outlined and
  its sketch in `energy`, the other three in muted ink. On Figure 16.12 +
  16.13 the dashed ΔH level, the shaded −TΔS wedge and its bracket are
  `energy` like the ΔG line, told apart by dashing, fill and label; on
  Figure 16.14 the tangent whose slope is ΔG is `energy` like the curve.
  ΔG₁° and ΔG₂° are not drawn and carry no `ref`.

The frame of a figure and its labels stay in ink, and with colour coding off
every figure stays legible from its labels.
